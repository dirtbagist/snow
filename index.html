How to read this

Snow is inches per day, shown as two models: NBM (NOAA's National Blend of Models) / ECMWF. When both agree, trust it more. A spread like 2 / 11 means the storm is uncertain.

Storm alert: 10"+ on the higher model. Watch: 6 to 9". Storms group consecutive days with 0.5"+ on either model.

Freezing level is the height where air hits 32°F. Snow usually reaches about 1,000 ft below it. If it sits near or below base elevation during precip, expect rain at the base.

Forecast points use each spot's terrain elevation, not the summit. Data: Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), free and keyless.
